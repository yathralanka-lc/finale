## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

**YathraLanka** ("Journey of Lanka") transforms passive tourism into an active, gamified cultural preservation movement. By combining real-time GPS geofencing, multimodal vision AI verification, and cryptographic state ledgers, YathraLanka rewards tourists for physically exploring Sri Lanka’s natural and historical landmarks while providing verified foot traffic to local businesses and actionable conservation data to heritage authorities.

## 2. Problem Statement & Market Pain Points

1. **Passive Tourism & Value Leakage:** Tourists spend 85% of their budget at isolated resorts without directly benefiting local heritage communities or small vendors.
2. **Vandalism & Unmonitored Heritage Degradation:** Central preservation authorities lack real-time visibility into physical site degradation across 1,000+ scattered ruins and nature reserves.
3. **Lack of Gen-Z / Millennial Cultural Engagement:** Traditional museums and static audio guides fail to captivate modern travelers seeking interactive, gamified experiences.
4. **Fraudulent Verification in Eco-Tourism:** Existing travel apps rely on unverified manual check-ins, susceptible to GPS spoofing and fake reviews.

### B2B Merchant Subscription Tiers

1. **Heritage Partner Tier ($49/month):**
   - Verified location pin on interactive map.
   - Up to 2 active promotional discount coupons unlocked via user XP.
   - Monthly foot-traffic analytics report.
2. **Cultural Hub Tier ($99/month):**
   - Priority map highlight and custom side quest placement (e.g., "Taste Traditional Spice Tea at Elahera").
   - Unlimited coupon unlocks and direct push notifications to nearby verified explorers.
3. **Enterprise Destination Tier ($199/month):**
   - Dedicated AR/Vision AI checkpoint integration.
   - Co-branded travel poster inclusion and real-time visitor heatmap API access.

PHASE 3: South Asian Expansion (Months 19 - 36)
