## Slide-by-Slide Script & Presenter Instructions

### Slide 1: Hook & The Cultural Crisis (Time: 0:00 - 1:00)
**Visual:** High-contrast split screen showing damaged ancient rock carvings alongside thousands of tourists taking selfies without engaging with local culture.

**Presenter Script:**
> *"Honorable Judges and fellow innovators, Sri Lanka welcomes over 2 million international visitors every year. They come for our ancient fortresses, sacred temples, and pristine rainforests. But behind the postcard pictures lies a silent crisis.*
> 
> *Tourists spend 85% of their money inside isolated resorts, leaving local merchants and heritage artisans behind. Worse yet, our 2,500-year-old landmarks suffer from unmonitored foot traffic and vandalism. Traditional travel apps treat tourism as passive consumption. Today, we change that forever. Welcome to YathraLanka—where we turn tourists into active guardians of our heritage."*

---

### Slide 2: The Solution & Value Proposition (Time: 1:00 - 2:00)
**Visual:** Clean 3D product mockup showing the YathraLanka app interface, interactive map pins, and gamified XP progress rings.

**Presenter Script:**
> *"YathraLanka is Sri Lanka’s first offline-first, gamified cultural verification platform. We bridge the physical and digital worlds using three core pillars:*
> 
> *First: **Gamified Exploration**—Tourists complete curated heritage trails, earn XP, unlock collectible ranks, and redeem rewards at local businesses.*
> *Second: **Verified Proof-of-Presence**—Using multi-sensor fusion and vision AI, we prove users are physically standing at the landmark.*
> *Third: **Direct Economic Impact**—We drive foot traffic directly to small local vendors, hoteliers, and community cleanup projects."*

**Presenter Script:**

> 
> *While apps like Pokémon GO gamify fiction, and AllTrails maps raw terrain, **YathraLanka is the only platform combining real-world vision AI, GPS anti-spoofing, and cryptographic state ledgers**. We aren't just an app; we are an unforgeable proof-of-presence network."*

### Slide 4: Engineering Architecture & Anti-Spoofing Engine (Time: 3:00 - 4:30)
**Visual:** Interactive Mermaid architecture diagram showing the Capacitor Native Bridge, Haversine Engine, Vision AI Inspection Module, and State Ledger.

**Presenter Script:**
> *"Let's talk engineering. Fake check-ins ruin gamification. To prevent GPS spoofing apps and photo forgery, we built a 4-layer security stack:*
> 
> *1. **Haversine Geofencing Engine:** Calculates exact distance deltas down to sub-10 meter resolution.*
> *2. **Velocity Sensor Guard:** Tracks speed vectors. If a user tries to mock jump coordinates at over 120 km/h, our system instantly flags `SPOOF_SUSPECTED`.*
> *3. **Dual-Model Vision AI:** Compares captured photo features against historical landmark patterns with real-time confidence scores.*
> *4. **Immutable State Ledger:** Every single verification generates a signed, timestamped block with SHA-256 seal signatures, stored locally first and synced upon network recovery."*
